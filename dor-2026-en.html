30-second executive pitch

DÖR 2026: a voluntary pilot for diaspora visibility, renewal through young operators and stronger community autonomy.

DÖR 2026 is a living diaspora-relationship pilot. Its purpose is to make Hungarian communities abroad visible, reachable and operable as a Hungarian partner network with young contributors - while fully preserving their own autonomy. It does not collect data about the diaspora; it builds living relationships with the diaspora: events, community stories, young operators, cultural presence and local connection points become visible.

Important starting point: this is not a purely theoretical model. A small-scale Vienna experience has already provided operational evidence: events, community communication, youth involvement, online payment, volunteer work, institutional cooperation and visible Hungarian community presence worked in a real environment.

In 30 seconds: the first DÖR pilot shows how 10-20 diaspora organisations can be connected into one voluntary, visible Hungarian community space that can be operated with young contributors. The first operating elements are: organisation profile, event calendar, Junior Hub connection and operating feedback. The system does not appoint representatives, does not rank communities, does not distribute funding and does not collect political data.

Which pain does it solve?Organisations are often invisible, overloaded, short of succession and without a shared operating picture.
What is the first product?Profile + event calendar + Junior Hub + basic operating feedback in a Wix-based pilot environment.
Why is it defensible?Voluntary, non-personalised, not ranking, not distributing funding and not collecting political data.
What is the decision?A factual Go / Modify / Stop decision after 90-120 days.
What proves it?The operating principle already exists on a small scale, so the logic can be demonstrated in person.
Executive interpretation

Two principles: autonomy and living connection.

Autonomy safeguard
DÖR does not appoint representatives, rank communities, distribute funding, penalise non-participation or collect political/worldview data. Participation is voluntary, reversible and does not affect the organisation’s own operation.
Living diaspora relationship
DÖR does not collect data about the diaspora; it builds a living relationship with the diaspora by making active organisations, events, young operators, local knowledge and community stories visible.
Executive sentence: DÖR 2026 is designed to make Hungarian communities abroad visible as reachable, youth-operated partner networks while preserving their autonomy.
Autonomy clause

Not a system above communities, but a voluntary operating infrastructure.

Operating feedback, not state classification
Operating feedback is primarily a self-development tool for the organisation. The programme owner can only see aggregated operating signals for pilot evaluation.
No funding pre-filter
The pilot result cannot be used directly for funding decisions, withdrawal of support, political classification or community ranking.
No legitimacy decision
DÖR does not decide which organisation is the “real” Hungarian community in a city or country.
Final autonomy sentence: We do not control. We do not rank. We do not distribute funding. We do not decide legitimacy. We do not collect political data.
Foreign-relations value

The diaspora should not be administered; it should be brought into living relationship.

Visible Hungarian presence
Organisational profiles, events, schools, clubs, cultural programmes and community stories become searchable in one place.
Youth connection points
The Junior Hub treats young people as operators, not as a passive target group: content, events, archives, interviews, translations and digital profiles.
Cultural and community diplomacy
DÖR does not replace diplomatic missions, but helps identify active community nodes and organisations suitable for dialogue.
Summary sentence: DÖR 2026 is not designed to administer the diaspora, but to create living relationships with it.
01

The diaspora problem is not technological. It is a visibility, succession and operating problem.

The project is understandable only if every level is clear: who has the pain, why it hurts, and what DÖR solves.

Pain 1 · community autonomy
Organisations do not want top-down control.
Many diaspora communities are sensitive to systems that define from above who represents whom.
DÖR
Voluntary infrastructure, not top-down control.
The organisation decides on joining, its profile, delegate, exit and communication.
Pain 2 · living connection
Diaspora work is often invisible.
Events, stories and volunteer work often remain inside local channels.
DÖR
A shared digital space with local autonomy.
The pilot provides profiles, an event calendar and a community news layer without replacing existing channels.
02

The first 90-120 days prove one thing only: does the minimum viable system work?

The pilot is not the full DÖR, not a funding system, not a global database and not a business platform. It tests four basic functions with real communities.

1 · Organisation profileWho the organisation is, where it operates, what channels it uses and who the contact person is.
2 · Event calendarAt least one event or news item uploaded through a simple publishing process.
3 · Junior HubA young editor is appointed, receives a mentored task list and portfolio confirmation.
4 · Operating reportNot a score and not a ranking, but operating feedback at the end of the pilot.

What is not part of the first pilot?

No funding scoreOperating feedback does not distribute money and does not rank communities.
No political dataNo party preference, worldview, voting data or loyalty information.
No mandatory participationNon-participating organisations may not suffer any disadvantage.
No global roll-outEvery continuation requires a separate decision.
No live business moduleThe Market Bridge can only be examined in a later validated phase.
No supplier lock-inThe technical structure must be documented, transferable and auditable.
03/A

Do not build a final state system first. Prove the operating principle quickly.

The goal is not to build a final global platform, but to prove whether organisations can manage their profiles, publish events, involve young editors and operate within controlled legal and financial frames.

100 editor access

Central money flow

Trained local owners

Transferable operation
The structure can be documented, trained and later transferred to mentored teams.

Autonomy and dialogue

Youth renewal

06

Not a new foundation. A ministry-supervised pilot programme.

The most defensible model is a time-limited, auditable pilot programme structure: policy supervision, a programme office, steering board and diaspora roundtable.

Recommended form: DÖR Pilot Programme Office + Steering Board

Daily execution is coordinated by a small office and supervised by a multi-stakeholder board.

Supervision logic

The mandate is not operational interference, but a guarantee: responsible owner, budget line, data protection frame, decision gate and stop right.

07

The pilot can only start if structural safeguards close the attack surfaces.

Public-finance firewall
No automatic extension, no automatic roll-out and no supplier privilege.
Data protection-political firewall
No political opinions, party preference, worldview data or loyalty information.
Voluntariness firewall
Non-participation may not cause any disadvantage.
Autonomy firewall
The platform does not decide legitimacy.
GOContinue only with validated use, voluntary participation and clean audit.
MODIFYModify if there is value but scope, cost, data protection or governance need correction.
STOPStop if community value is not proven or risk cannot be closed.
07/B

The project is defensible when seven decision-making logics lead to the same conclusion: small, measurable, clean, voluntary and stoppable.

Transparency control
Who receives money, why, for how long and with what control?
Autonomy control
Will this become top-down diaspora control?
Living connection control
Does the diaspora receive an actual role?
Startup control
What is the smallest provable product?
Product / fintech control
Does it work for payments and permissions?
Reputation control
Could it look like an influence or data-collection system?
08

It does not start from theory. There is an operating sample.

The Vienna experience shows that a small team, community trust and digital structure can create measurable growth, multi-generational involvement and media-visible results.

10

Cost, staffing and timeline - expandable.

The Wix fee is only one technical base cost. Build, training, operation, data protection documentation, bookkeeping control and support must be planned separately.
